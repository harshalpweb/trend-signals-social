What a return really costs you. Most sellers only count the lost margin. The real number is bigger.

On a ₹599 sale: ₹49 forward shipping, ₹65 reverse shipping, ₹15 repackaging, and roughly ₹30 in damage risk. That's ₹159 gone, 27% of the sale, on a single return.

Three ways to cut it: add real size and fit photos (most apparel RTOs are size mismatches, not damage), call high-value COD orders before dispatch, and track your RTO rate per pincode since some zones run 3x the average.
